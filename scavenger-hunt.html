Good to know

Do I need to book a time slot?

No. Play any day, at any hour, and pause or resume as you like. There is a full refund until you start playing.

What do I need to bring?

A smartphone with the Questo app (iOS or Android), comfortable shoes, and a charged battery or a power bank, since the quest runs on your phone.

Is the route accessible?

The route has uneven terrain and some stairs, so it is not wheelchair accessible.
